# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-14

- A **Sound** key on the alarm's card takes the next sound and plays it once; a sound chosen in Home Assistant plays once too.
- The line under the time says when the alarm rings and counts down to it every second (Daily - 7:00 AM - in 8:12:40).
- The tile shows its name above the time, and a tile two cells high gives the countdown a line of its own.
- On a small screen the tile and the card leave out what has no room instead of drawing over each other.
- A waiting snooze shows in amber and counts down too (Snoozed until 7:09 AM - in 8:40).
- An item for the top bar: an alarm clock, green with the alarm's time while it is on and grey while it is off.
- An alarm clock icon beside the time on the alarm's card.
- The tile and the card say **Off** in red, or **Daily** or **Weekdays** in green with when it rings next.
- Turning the alarm off, on the screen or from Home Assistant, also ends a ring or a waiting snooze.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-14

- Four sounds to choose from with **Alarm tone**: Beeps, Gentle chime, Rising and Classic alarm.
- **Cancel snooze** on the alarm's card while a snooze waits, and **Alarm snooze minutes** sets how long a snooze
  lasts (9 minutes unless you change it).
- The popup shows a red bell that pulses, with rings rippling out of it.
- The card that sets the alarm says what time it is now.
- Snooze and Stop no longer let the tap through to the tile under the popup.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-14

- The first version: an alarm set on the screen or from Home Assistant, Snooze and Stop, weekdays only, and beeps on a
  screen with a speaker.
